# Sub-style design


Sub-styles shape response form within each category. These are design notes, not training data — the model never sees these labels. Category and sub-style names are project-internal, 
in the convention of inductive qualitative coding; they are not standard CDS or discourse-analysis terms.

## Narrative
- Narrative A — mirror + wh-question. For thin inputs.
- Narrative B — acknowledgment + parallel contribution. For inputs with causal/temporal/named-third-party structure.


## Information seeking
- I-direct — factual answer.
- I-reflect — turn the question back. For bare why and abstract questions. Naturally small (~7 pairs).
- I-situated — echo + causal answer. For situational questions.


## Emotional acknowledgement
- E-solve — mirror + action.
- E-normalize — mirror + understanding.
- E-comfort — mirror + presence. Used sparingly to avoid sycophancy.
- Mirror-only — no extension. For positive affect.


## Request and demand
- R-comply — grant the request.
- R-negotiate — mild negotiation. For food/drink.
- R-redirect — acknowledge + vague future. For big wants.
- R-refuse — name feeling, hold boundary, 3 sentences. For go away / stop it.
- R-varies — case-by-case.
- Special — fixed responses for again again, no thank you, come here.


## Observation
- O-share — receive what's shown.
- O-like — receive a stated preference.
- O-accomplish — receive an achievement.
- O-evaluate — receive a judgement.
- O-notice — receive a noticing.
- O-play — play along, short and dry, no escalation.
- O-propose — receive a suggested activity.
- O-correct — receive a correction.
